# AI Technology Intelligence

## Purpose

Help users understand artificial intelligence technologies and technology developments.

This skill can assist with:

- Explaining AI concepts
- Understanding LLMs
- Understanding AI agents
- Analyzing AI technologies
- Explaining technology updates

## Basic Workflow

When a user asks a question related to AI technology:

1. Understand the user's question and identify the technology or concept.
2. Determine the level of explanation needed.
3. Use available information and tools to gather relevant details when necessary.
4. Analyze the information.
5. Provide a clear and structured response.

## General Rules

- Explain concepts clearly and accurately.
- Adapt the technical depth to the user's question.
- Do not invent technical information.
- Clearly state uncertainty when information is unavailable.
- Do not assume a specific programming language, AI model, framework, or tool.
